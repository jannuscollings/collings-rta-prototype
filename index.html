Contract of Sale

  • Observed on Mac. The Sales row offered a contract download. Choosing No opened Private Sales. The dummy $500,000 offer saved as DRAFT. RTA said it would stay in draft until a contract was loaded against the listing.
  • Offer attachment verified on Mac. Add Document → Choose PDFs saved the mock PDF on the draft. View Document reopened it. Contract Status remained Not Generated and Preview Contract was unavailable.
  • RTA training material. The private treaty example shows the agent preparing and previewing the contract, sending it for purchaser offsite signing, and opening vendor in-room signing. When the price changes, the purchaser receives a new link to initial it. The separate auction contract example shows purchaser portal consent, signature, and an option to send copies to the parties.
  • Open checks. Verify the Mac path from Yes through preview, save, and reopening the contract. Capture the private treaty signer screens and agent status after each action. Confirm what triggers exchange, how each party retrieves the final copy, and when configured post-sale actions run.

Auction bid recording

  • Observed on Mac. The row action Start Auction opened the operator screen. A second Start Auction started the timer. The Unique bidders plus raised the count from 0 to 1 without opening a bidder selector. The operator offered Hand writing and Number pad.
  • Observed on 24 November. The paused test auction reopened with $5 current and empty history. The operator's Continue Auction restarted the timer. +500 made $505 current and moved $5 into history. Delete Last Bid restored $5 and empty history. Back → Pause Auction → Yes returned to Auction Paused.
  • Documented by RTA. Bidder Registration assigns a paddle number. Request follow auction link sets up a live bid screen and sends an email. The dummy Mac menu showed Request follow auction link, while Bidder Registration was absent.
  • Open checks. Verify multi-digit opening entry, bidder attribution, older-row correction, the Streaming audience, and what PASSED IN saves, publishes or notifies. A matching Results entry has yet to be verified.
